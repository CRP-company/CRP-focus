but why?
Research shows that notifications can disrupt attention and task performance - even when people don't check their phones. Studies also show that peaple interact with their phones about every five minutes, while interruptions can increase stress and negatively affect productivity well-being. Simply turning notifications off isn't always the answer: research has found that completely disabling alerts can increase anxiety and the fear of missing something important. CRP Focus takes a different approach. Instead of showing you everything or hiding everything, CRP focus filters your notifications and shows you what really matters - helping you reduce interruptions and regain control of your attention.
